## Notes on data gaps

- ENS is the most transparently reported protocol due to DAO governance and public forums.
- Handshake HNS burn data is the best on-chain activity proxy for Handshake (no registration fees in the traditional sense).
- SPACE ID, Unstoppable Domains, and 3DNS do not publish detailed revenue reports publicly.
- Daily resolution query counts are rarely reported by any protocol; ENS's 3.2M figure is an exception.
